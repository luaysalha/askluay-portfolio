Supporting Lina Sanjab’s documentary through footage preparation and editing work in Lebanon.

My contribution

In Lebanon, I collaborated with director Lina Sanjab on The Madness of Aleppo, helping prepare and cut material from hundreds of hours of footage. My contribution supported the editing process.

About the film

The documentary follows medical workers and civilians around Al-Quds Hospital during the siege of Aleppo in 2016.
