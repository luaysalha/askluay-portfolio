About

Hello, I'm Luay.

I'm a visual designer and editor with more than ten years across video, motion and graphic design. I keep brands consistent everywhere they appear, and build the templates and asset libraries that let a whole team stay on brand without me in the room.

I've edited humanitarian and journalistic films for Al Jazeera and the BBC, run brand and communications for an international NGO, and now design social-first campaigns and build digital tools in The Hague. I work happily across cultures, in Arabic, English and improving Dutch.
